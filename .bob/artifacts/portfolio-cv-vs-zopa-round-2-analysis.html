What still needs attention

🔴 Native mobile — still absent
Nothing has changed here. Zopa rebuilt their system for native. This is the one gap that can't be closed within the portfolio.
Action: Address directly in your cover letter. Frame it honestly: all your systems work has been web/enterprise, but system strategy, governance, adoption and engineering partnership all transfer. The role's leadership requirements don't change by surface.
🟡 "System health" language still missing from the migration case study
The compliance rubric is now described well — "a three-tier rubric across six criteria that gave teams a clear target and leadership a measurable view of progress." But the Zopa JD specifically says "define and track measures of system health, adoption, consistency and impact." You built exactly that tool. One sentence naming it as such would make the connection explicit for a reviewer scanning for that language.
Action: In the "Holding the Bar" section, add one sentence: something like "It became our first measure of system health across eight products — a consistent, trackable definition of readiness that teams and leadership could both use."
🟡 Figma kit — one human moment still missing
The new paragraph is good but ends at the team-as-output level ("it delivered"). What's still absent is one moment of actual people management: a designer who struggled, a priority conflict you navigated, a capability gap you closed. Zopa is hiring someone to lead and develop a small team — they will ask about this in interview, and a specific example now would help you prepare.
Action: One more sentence in "How We Built It" — something like "When one designer's product deadlines pulled them off the kit entirely, I redistributed the components and took on the most complex ones myself." That's a people management decision, not just a delivery one.
🔵 Hero body text — "five awards" mention is worth reconsidering
The current hero copy says "earning five awards specifically for it." The awards are now covered by the trophy stat in the hero bar — so this is slightly redundant. More importantly, for a Lead role, leading with awards can read as individual contribution rather than leadership. The sentence does useful work contextualising the Carbon background, but the awards mention could be swapped for something that signals strategy or scale.
Suggestion: Replace "earning five awards specifically for it" with something like "leading adoption across acquired products at scale" — which is actually a stronger leadership signal for this role.
